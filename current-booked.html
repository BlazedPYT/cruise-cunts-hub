Norwegian Jewel

Norwegian Jewel is one of Norwegian Cruise Line's Jewel-class ships, giving the group a smaller and more traditional cruise-ship experience than NCL's newest mega-ships.

This itinerary has a nice balance of island stops and sea days, with ports in the Bahamas, Dominican Republic, Aruba, Curaçao, Jamaica, and Grand Cayman.

There are three sea days built into the sailing, which gives everybody plenty of time to actually enjoy the ship instead of waking up for a port every single morning.
